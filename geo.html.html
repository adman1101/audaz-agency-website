What AI Looks For / Lo que busca la IA

THE SIGNALS
THAT MATTER.

Las señales que importan.

01
Consistent Brand Entity
Entidad de marca consistente

Your name, category, and description must match perfectly across every source. Conflicts = invisible. Consistency = recommended.

02
Structured Content
Contenido estructurado

AI favors FAQ sections, question-based headers, and answer-first writing — not keyword-stuffed paragraphs built for 2005 Google.

03
RAG-Ready Formatting
Formato listo para IA

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) means AI pulls live data. Your content must be indexed, semantically relevant, and extractable as citable claims.

04
Cross-Platform Mentions
Menciones en múltiples plataformas

AI values external validation over internal links. We get your brand mentioned where AI trains and retrieves: trusted publications, forums, and knowledge bases.

05
Semantic Relevance
Relevancia semántica

AI matches by meaning, not keywords. Your content must answer the exact questions your customers ask AI — in the natural language they use.

06
Schema Markup
Marcado de esquema

Structured data markup tells AI exactly who you are, what you do, where you are, and who you serve. Without it, AI guesses — often wrong.
