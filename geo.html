Core Definitions

The Two Pillars of AI Visibility

Most agencies don't know these exist yet. AUDAZ has been implementing both since day one.

GEO
GEO
Generative Engine Optimization

Generative Engine Optimization is the process of optimizing digital content so that artificial intelligence models — such as ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity — cite and recommend your brand in synthesized search results. Unlike traditional SEO which targets blue links, GEO targets the AI-generated answers that now appear before any search results.

AEO
AEO
Answer Engine Optimization

Answer Engine Optimization focuses on structuring content into concise, authoritative answers designed for voice assistants and zero-click search results, prioritizing immediate user intent over traditional link clicks. AEO uses schema markup, FAQ structure, and direct-answer formatting to get featured in AI Overviews and voice search responses.
